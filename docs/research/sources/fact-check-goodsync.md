## Other errors noticed

- None of the 12 claims is wrong. Minor nuances found while checking:
  - Claim 2's "$29.95" is the USD price; the store may display €29.95 by default depending on locale/currency.
  - Claim 6 is slightly incomplete: Sib-SMB (`smbd://`) is also shipped on Windows (where the native Win-SMB `smb://` alternative exists); it is not Mac/Linux-only.
  - Trial length is 15 days for Personal/Workstation/Server, but the Backup Control Center trial is only 14 days — don't generalize "15-day" to all products.
  - The free edition is additionally limited to 0 simultaneous connections (Personal = 4), per the Personal comparison table.
  - Claim 9: "Rename Losing File, not Delete" only takes effect when "Automatically Resolve Conflicts (strong ones)" is enabled; it is unchecked by default, and when the winning side deletes the file no Conflicted Copy is made.
