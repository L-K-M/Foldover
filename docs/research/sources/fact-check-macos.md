## Other errors noticed

- Claim 6 wording nuance: Apple says signing "turns on automatically when needed if the server offers it" — the trigger is server capability/negotiation, not literally a server *requirement*.
- Claim 9 precision: HFS+ does not store literal NFD — it stores a Unicode 3.2 Form D variant with some character substitutions excluded. Functionally decomposed, colloquially "NFD".
- Claim 9 timeline nuance: iOS 10.3 shipped APFS first but normalization-*sensitive*; normalization-insensitivity arrived with iOS 10.3.3/10.12.6 (runtime) and iOS 11/macOS 10.13 (native). The claim's "10.13/iOS 11" refers to the native scheme — correct, but incomplete.
- Claim 4 precision: Sequoia 15.0–15.3 kept *both* rsync implementations with a runtime selector; only in 15.4 was samba-rsync deleted. "Replaced" happened at 15.0 for the default, "removed" at 15.4.
- Claim 7 precision: inside the bundle the plist must use `BundleProgram` (relative to the bundle) instead of `Program`, and lives specifically in `Contents/Library/LaunchAgents`.
- Claim 12 precision: the prompt also covers launch agents; launch daemons running as root are exempt.
- Claim 1 context: Golden Gate is the first macOS to drop Intel Macs entirely and the last to support Rosetta 2.
